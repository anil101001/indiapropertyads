IndiaPropertyAds CRM Roadmap

From Property Listings to Complete Sales Platform

Vision

Transform IndiaPropertyAds from a property listing portal into a complete real estate sales platform that helps sellers manage inquiries, nurture buyers, and close deals — all in one place.

Phase 1: Foundation CRM

"Never Miss a Lead"

Features

Feature What It Does
Inquiry Dashboard Centralized view of all buyer inquiries for sellers
Lead Status Pipeline Track each inquiry through stages: New → Contacted → Interested → Site Visit → Negotiation → Closed
Contact Logging Record calls, messages, and notes for each lead
Follow-up Reminders Automated alerts for pending follow-ups
Quick Response One-click call/WhatsApp/email buttons

Benefits

  • Sellers never lose track of interested buyers
  • All inquiries organized in one place instead of scattered across email and WhatsApp
  • Automatic reminders ensure timely follow-ups
  • Clear visibility into which leads are progressing
  • Faster response times lead to higher conversions

Phase 2: Smart CRM

"Focus on the Right Buyers"

Features

Feature What It Does
Lead Scoring Automatically rank buyers (0-100) based on their activity and engagement
Buyer Intent Signals Show sellers when a buyer viewed, saved, or compared their property
Smart Property Matching Auto-suggest properties to buyers based on their search history and preferences
Site Visit Scheduling Built-in calendar for booking property visits
Automated Nurturing Auto-send property updates, price drops, and similar listings to interested buyers
Bulk Actions Message multiple leads at once, update statuses in bulk

Benefits

  • Sellers focus energy on serious buyers, not tire-kickers
  • Real buyer behavior data replaces guesswork
  • Automated follow-ups keep buyers engaged without manual effort
  • Streamlined scheduling eliminates back-and-forth
  • Personalized recommendations increase buyer satisfaction
  • Time savings when managing multiple leads

Phase 3: Pro CRM

"Run Your Real Estate Business"

Features

Feature What It Does
WhatsApp Business Integration Send automated messages, updates, and reminders via WhatsApp
Negotiation Tracker Log offers, counter-offers, and track negotiation history
Document Vault Securely store and share ID proofs, agreements, and property documents
Team Collaboration Add team members, assign leads, and track team performance
Advanced Analytics Conversion funnels, response time analysis, and performance dashboards
Channel Partner Portal Brokers can access inventory, submit leads, and track commissions
Payment Tracking Track token amounts, payment milestones, and schedules
Mobile App Full CRM functionality on mobile for on-the-go management

Benefits

  • Reach buyers on their preferred channel (WhatsApp)
  • Complete deal history from first inquiry to final payment
  • Organized paperwork reduces friction in closing
  • Scale from individual seller to team or agency
  • Data-driven insights for better business decisions
  • Expand sales reach through broker network
  • Manage entire business from anywhere

Competitive Advantage

Most property portals (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing) only provide listings — sellers receive inquiries via email and must manage everything themselves.

IndiaPropertyAds with CRM becomes the only platform offering:

  • Property listings AND built-in sales management
  • End-to-end journey from listing to closing
  • Tools that help sellers succeed, not just advertise

This creates a unique market position and strong seller loyalty.
